Reimagining quick loans for small businesses in the UAE

QFunder is Tradeling’s new fintech vertical. Banks rarely lend to small businesses because they see them as high risk, and when they do, it takes weeks. We built an MVP to test a faster way: apply online in minutes, get funded in 24 hours. Our approach was build fast, fail fast.

The opportunity

Small businesses wait weeks for a loan. Many never get one.

In the UAE, small and medium businesses are most of the market but get only a small share of bank credit.

  1. Banks see small businesses as high risk.

    So loans are hard to get: a long track record, a long review, and many businesses are turned down.

  2. Applying takes weeks.

    Branch visits, paper documents and manual checks, before anyone says yes or no.

  3. Traders need the money now.

    They borrow to buy stock. If the money arrives too late, the deal is gone.

Product strategy

Two months to test the idea. So we took out every manual check.

Manual checks were what stood between a business and its money. I pushed for automating every one of them for the MVP.

The design

Built to earn trust, and to move fast.

  1. Landing page
  2. Onboarding
  3. Application
  4. Dashboard

Trust comes first.

For a new lender, trust is everything. I kept the page institutional but easy to read: no jargon, three clear promises (fast, simple, secure) and a loan calculator that shows the monthly payment before a business signs up.

qfunder.tradeling.com

Easy to finish, hard to fake.

Make onboarding too hard and businesses drop off. Make it too easy and we risk fraud. I broke one long form into short steps, with KYC verified instantly.

qfunder.tradeling.com

Apply in 3–5 minutes.

Simple enough to finish in 3–5 minutes. I removed the extra steps banks ask for out of habit and kept only the essential data.

qfunder.tradeling.com

Never in the dark.

A business sees its repayment schedule, its next payment and its payment history, and can pay from the same place. It always knows what it owes, so it can plan its cash flow.

Where it ended

Built and tested in two months.

We built the MVP and tested it with users. It did not launch, for reasons unrelated to the product work.

Lessons

Perfect is the enemy of good. We used Shufti Pro for KYC/KYB checks and an open-source loan management system (LMS), instead of building our own. For an MVP, that is a valid design decision: test the idea first, build later.

Trust is visual. In fintech, clean design is not only about looks. If a screen looks broken or cheap, people will not trust it with their financial data.
